## 🌐 About TSPL Network

**TSPL Network** is a technology-focused organization building digital products, services, and experiences for businesses, creators, and individuals.

We combine software development, modern web technologies, thoughtful design, and creative problem-solving to turn ideas into reliable digital solutions.

Our work spans from custom websites and software to internal tools, platforms, and experimental projects.

### 📖 Our History

TSPL Network was originally founded in **2021** and evolved over the following years alongside its projects and ideas.

In **late 2025**, the organization was renamed to **TSPL Network**, marking the beginning of a new direction focused on digital products, services, technology, and creative solutions.

While the name and direction evolved, the underlying idea remained the same:

> **Imagine the possibilities.**

## 🏗️ Our Approach

We believe technology should support an idea rather than restrict it.

Every project has different requirements, limitations, and goals. Instead of forcing everything into the same template, we build around the individual needs of each project.

**Understand → Design → Build → Improve**

---

## 🌱 Responsible Use of Resources

Building digital products also means using physical and digital resources.

We aim to use resources **consciously and efficiently** wherever practical.

This includes considering:

* ⚡ Energy-efficient infrastructure where possible
* 🖥️ Responsible use of computing resources
* ☁️ Efficient hosting and server utilization
* ♻️ Extending the useful life of hardware where practical
* 📦 Avoiding unnecessary physical materials
* 🧹 Keeping digital systems efficient and avoiding unnecessary resource consumption

We don't believe that every project needs to make unrealistic environmental claims. Instead, we focus on making **practical improvements where we can**.

---

## 🔐 Privacy & Security
